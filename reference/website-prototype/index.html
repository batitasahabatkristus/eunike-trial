Who we are

Family is the first place faith takes root.

Yayasan Eunike is an interdenominational Christian ministry focused on family discipleship. We partner with families so faith can be formed intentionally and passed from one generation to the next.

Our conviction

A strong church begins with a strong home.

We believe parents are the primary educators and spiritual leaders of their children. Eunike exists to equip, mentor, and build supportive communities around that calling.
